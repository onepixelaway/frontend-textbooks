The Deliberate Life / Action02

A four-week operating rhythm

The 30-Day Agency Plan

Control grows when your environment remembers what matters before your mood has to.

Each week adds one durable layer. Keep the practices small enough to repeat and visible enough to review.

01Week one

Clear the field

Audit obligations, pause low-value inputs, and write a one-sentence definition of “enough” for this month.

Daily practice
10-minute shutdown
02Week two

Protect the vital hour

Place one high-agency block before communication. Give it a location, start cue, and visible finish line.

Daily practice
60-minute focus block
03Week three

Practice clean decisions

Replace automatic yeses with a pause. Compare every new request against the month’s declared priority.

Decision rule
Trade, defer, or decline
04Week four

Lock in the system

Review what worked, remove friction from the winning habits, and schedule the next monthly reset.

Weekly practice
Friday agency review
